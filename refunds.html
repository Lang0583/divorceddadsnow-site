Refund policy

Last updated: October 2026

If the PDF isn't what you needed, email us within 30 days of purchase and we'll refund you in full. No hoops.

Email hello@divorceddadsnow.com from the address you used at checkout, or include that address in your note. Refunds go back to your original payment method through Stripe. Your bank may take 5 to 10 business days to show it.

File problems

If your download is broken, missing, or didn't arrive, tell us and we'll send it again right away.

Amazon and other stores

Kindle and paperback purchases, and purchases from other retailers, are refunded under that retailer's policy. We can't process those refunds directly.
