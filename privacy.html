Privacy

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

When you buy the PDF. Checkout is handled by Stripe. Stripe collects your payment details; we never see or store your full card number. Stripe shares with us what we need to fill your order: your name, email address, billing country or postal code, and the details of the purchase.

When you email us. We use what you send to answer you.

When you visit the site. There's no account registration, no advertising tracking, and no analytics on this site. Our hosting and security providers process standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) and may set a strictly necessary security cookie to protect the site from abuse.

How we use it

To deliver your purchase by email, answer support questions, process refunds, prevent fraud, and keep the records the law requires. We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't use your purchase email for marketing unless you ask to hear from us.

Who helps us

Stripe (payments), Google (email delivery), our order automation provider (which passes your email address from Stripe to our mailer), and our website host. Each processes information only to provide its service to us.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept as long as needed for tax, accounting, and support. Support emails are kept as long as they're useful for helping you.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct, or delete the information we hold about you, subject to records we must keep. Email hello@divorceddadsnow.com.

Children

This site and book are written for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we add a newsletter or analytics, we'll update this page before those features go live.
