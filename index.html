Common questions.

What is The Divorced Dad's Playbook?

The Divorced Dad's Playbook is a practical, child-centered guide for fathers going through separation and divorce. It walks you through the first week, hiring a lawyer, money, custody, parenting plans, communication, mediation, and life after the decree. Checklists, worksheets, and a 30-day stability plan are included.

Who is it for?

It is for dads who just got served, just had the conversation, or think it's coming, and for fathers already working through custody and co-parenting. It is written for dads who want to stay in their kids' lives without turning the family into a battleground.

Is this legal advice?

No. The book is educational information, not legal advice. Family law differs by state and by situation. Check anything that affects your case with a family law attorney licensed in your state. If you or your kids are in danger, call 911.
