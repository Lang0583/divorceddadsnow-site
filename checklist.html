The First 72 Hours

A one-page checklist for the first few days after it starts.

When it first hits, you can’t think straight. This puts the first few things on one page so you don’t have to.

  • Safety and your kids’ day-to-day needs first
  • Deadlines, hearings, and appointments written down
  • Your records gathered the right way
  • Your kids’ schedules, schools, and routines on paper
  • Questions ready for a family law attorney or mediator
Download the free checklist (PDF)

Free. No purchase and no email address needed. Print it, or tick the boxes on screen in Adobe Acrobat Reader.

Who it’s for

Dads who just got served, just had the conversation, or think it’s coming. It’s a printable one-page companion to the First 72 Hours bonus guide in The Divorced Dad’s Playbook.
